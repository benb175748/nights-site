If you break these rules, we remove the content and can remove you from Nights, without warning.

Reporting and blocking

  • We review every report within 24 hours. When content breaks these terms we remove it, and we remove the person who posted it from Nights.
  • You can block anyone. Once you block someone, you stop seeing each other's photos and reactions.

  • You can delete your own posts at any time, and group owners can delete any post in their group. Nights older than 12 months are deleted unless someone marked them as kept. The privacy policy explains what we store and for how long.
