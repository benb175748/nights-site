email us at benburgess1234@gmail.com and a real person will get back to you. tell us the email or Apple Account you sign in with and your username, and what happened. please don't send photos of other people unless we ask.

use the same address to report a photo or a person, to ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete something.

delete your account

you can do it yourself, in the app, at any time:

  1. on the home screen, tap your picture in the top right.
  2. scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. tap Delete everything to confirm.

this deletes your profile, every photo and video you posted, your reactions, your notification and lock screen tokens, and your group memberships, straight away. groups where you were the last member are deleted too; in other groups, ownership passes to the member who's been there longest. it can't be undone. if you can't get into the app any more, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll do it for you.

signing in

  • sign in with Apple or use email. there are no passwords.
  • with email, we send a link and a 6-digit code. type the code in the app, or open the link on the same phone. both work for an hour.
  • nothing arrived? check spam, then tap can't find it? send again. only the newest email works after that.
  • nights is for people 18 and over. the app won't create a profile for anyone younger.

groups and nights

  • to join a group, ask someone in it for its invite code (8 letters and numbers), then tap Join with a code on the home screen (or have a code? join a group if you have no groups yet). there's no search, so a code is the only way in.
  • group owners can rename the group, get a new code (the old one stops working), and remove people, from the group's members sheet.
  • anyone in the group can start or end a night. a night ends by itself after 12 hours.
  • if a night ends after midnight, its recap opens at 9 am. that's on purpose, so everyone sees it together.
  • nights are cleared after 12 months unless someone keeps them: open the night, tap its title, and turn on Keep this night.

something's wrong with a photo or a person

  • report a photo: in a recap, press and hold the photo (or tap ··· while watching), then Report. the group's owner sees it and can delete it.
  • block someone: same menu, Block, or from the group's members sheet. you'll stop seeing each other's photos and reactions.
  • unblock: tap your picture, then Blocked people.
  • delete your own photo: press and hold it, then Delete. it's gone for everyone.
  • your work used without permission: report it with the reason it's my work, used without permission, or email us. we remove content that infringes someone's rights (see the terms).

notifications and the lock screen

  • nights only sends three notifications: a friend started a night, a recap is ready, someone reacted to your photo (at most once an hour). turn them on or off from your picture → Notifications.
  • while a night is live, it can sit on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. switch it off any time from your picture → Lock screen.

more about what we store and why is in the privacy policy.
