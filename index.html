nights

a shared camera for your nights out.
private. no feed, no followers. 18+.

nights is a shared camera for you and the friends you go out with. start a night, and everyone in your group shoots photos and short videos into the same roll. the next morning, the recap is waiting. it's private by design: groups are invite-only, there's no public feed, nobody follows anybody, and your photos are only ever seen by the people in your group. nights is for adults, 18 and over, and it's on iPhone.
