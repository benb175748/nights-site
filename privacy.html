Privacy

Nights is a private app for a group of friends to document a night out. This page says, in plain language, what happens to your photos and your information. It is the same text inside the app and on the App Store listing.

Nights is run by Ben Burgess. When this page says "we", it means them.

Your photos and videos are private to your group

  • Anything you post to a Night can be seen only by the people who are members of that group. There is no public feed, no discovery, no way to follow someone, and no way to view a Night you were not part of.
  • Photos and videos are stored in a private bucket. The app fetches them with short-lived signed links (one hour) that only work for people who are signed in and in the group.
  • We never use your photos or videos for anything else. No advertising, no training of models, no analysis, no sharing with anyone outside your group.
  • Location data is removed from photos and videos on your phone before they are uploaded. We never receive where a photo was taken.

What we store

  • Account: the Apple Account or email you sign in with, your username, display name, optional avatar, and your birthdate. If you sign in with Apple and choose to share your name, it is used only to fill in your display name, which you can change before saving. The birthdate is used only to confirm you are 18 or older and is never shown to anyone.
  • Groups and Nights: which groups you are in, the Nights in those groups, and the photos, videos, captions, reactions, and timestamps you and your friends post.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications, a push token for your device, linked to your account, so we can tell you when a Night starts, when a recap is ready, or when someone reacted to your photo. That is the complete list of notifications; there are no others. Your phone's time zone is stored with the token so a recap that is ready overnight arrives at 9am your time, not in the middle of the night.
  • Lock screen tokens: if you turn on the lock screen display, push tokens from Apple for your device. See Lock screen below.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a photo or a person, or block someone, we keep a record so the group owner and we can act on it, and so the block stays in effect. Where the law requires it, we pass reported content and the account details behind it to the authorities; in particular, apparent child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and kept as the law requires.
  • Server logs: like any online service, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs of requests to our servers, including IP addresses and the kind of device or browser making them. They are used only to keep the service running and secure.

Lock screen

  • If you turn it on (the app asks once, and Settings has a Lock screen switch), a live Night shows on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island: its name, the group, how long it has left, how many photos are in it, how many people are taking them, and how many are yours.
  • To keep that up to date while the app is closed, we store push tokens that Apple gives your phone, linked to your account. One lets a Night a friend starts appear on your lock screen, with a notification, so it is used only if you have allowed notifications too; it is kept while the switch is on. The others belong to a single live Night and are used only to update it.
  • A Night's tokens are deleted when the Night ends or is deleted. All of your lock screen tokens are deleted when you turn the switch off, when you sign out, and when you delete your account. Tokens Apple says are no longer valid are deleted as well.
  • These updates go from our server straight to Apple's push service. They carry only what is shown on the lock screen, never your photos.

What we do not do

  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use analytics that identify you.
  • We do not read your camera roll. Importing from it only reads the files you pick and the time they were taken, to check they belong to the Night.

Who can see what

  • People in your group can see your display name, username, avatar, and everything you post to that group's Nights.
  • Nobody outside your groups can see your posts, your Nights or your groups. Your avatar is stored at a hard-to-guess link so it loads quickly; someone who somehow had that exact link could see the picture, but nothing else about you.
  • If someone leaves a group or is removed, they lose access to that group's Nights immediately.
  • If you block someone, you stop seeing each other's photos and reactions.

How long we keep things

  • Nights are kept for 12 months. After that, their photos and videos are deleted automatically unless someone in the group marked the Night as kept.
  • You can delete any of your own posts at any time. Group owners can delete any post in their group, including reported ones.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your profile, your posts and their files, your reactions, your device and lock screen tokens, and your memberships. Groups where you were the last member are deleted entirely. This cannot be undone.

Age

Nights is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and the app refuses to create a profile for anyone younger. The App Store rating is 18+.

Who handles your data

A few companies run parts of Nights for us. They get only what they need to do that job.

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, and your photos and videos, on servers in the United States. If you use Nights from another country, your data is sent to the United States and stored there, under Supabase's data processing terms.
  • An email delivery provider sends the sign-in emails with your code and link. It receives your email address and the message.
  • Expo and Apple deliver push notifications. They receive the notification text (for example "Sam reacted to your photo."), your device's push token, the kind of notification, and a link to the Night it is about. Expo also receives an identifier for this installation of the app when it issues the push token.
  • Apple delivers lock screen updates straight from our server. They carry what the lock screen shows (see Lock screen above), who started the Night when a friend starts one, and the lock screen tokens. Never your photos.

If a security incident affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Your rights

  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing the address below. We will reply within a month.
  • You can delete your whole account yourself at any time in Settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have other rights too, such as objecting to how we use your data or complaining to your data protection authority.

Terms

Using Nights also means agreeing to the terms of use, including zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour. They are in the app next to this page and on the Nights website.
